Quick Answers

Good to know.

Does the package include the Facebook/Instagram ad budget?

No. For Video packages (PRO/CINEMA) and Monthly packages, campaign setup and monitoring are included, but the amount paid to Meta for ad delivery (Ad Spend) is separate and provided by you.

Can I get both vertical and landscape formats?

One format (9:16 or 16:9) is included per video package. The second format can be added for an extra fee, confirmed before production.

When do I need to pay for the Monthly Packages?

Because we manage a large portfolio of pages, the full monthly package amount must be paid upfront before work commences for that month.

Who replies to customers in the Monthly Packages?

In the Gold and Platinum packages, Vibe Ads provides initial responses to inquiries and directs potential leads to you (the business owner). You are responsible for finalizing negotiations, sales, calls, and order fulfillment.

Are views, messages or sales guaranteed?

No. Advertising results vary based on the offer, creative, audience, budget, season and platform delivery. We do not promise a specific reach, message count or sales result.
