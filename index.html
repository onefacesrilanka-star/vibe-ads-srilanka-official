Quick Answers

Good to know.

Does the package include the Facebook/Instagram ad budget?

No. For VIBE PRO and VIBE CINEMA, campaign setup and 7-day monitoring are included, but the amount paid to Meta for ad delivery is separate and agreed with you.

Can I get both vertical and landscape formats?

One format (9:16 or 16:9) is included per package. The second format can be added for an extra fee, confirmed before production.

Can I change the script after production starts?

For VIBE PRO and VIBE CINEMA, the script/concept is approved before production. One round of minor final-video changes is included; a new script, new scenes or a major concept change may require an additional quote.

Are views, messages or sales guaranteed?

No. Advertising results vary based on the offer, creative, audience, budget, season and platform delivery. We do not promise a specific reach, message count or sales result.

Can VIBE CINEMA use my own story idea?

Yes. You can bring your own story or ask VIBE ADS to develop a new concept suited to your business and product.
